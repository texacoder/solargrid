Who We Are

Building a Sustainable Tomorrow, One Rooftop at a Time

Founded with a single goal — making solar energy accessible, dependable and genuinely worth it — SolarGrid Energy Solutions has grown into a trusted name for complete solar EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction) services across Kerala.

We don't just install panels. We design systems around your actual consumption, source components from proven manufacturers, and stay with you long after commissioning through ongoing monitoring and maintenance.
