Consultation & Design

We start by understanding your energy consumption, roof structure and budget. Our engineers assess site conditions and design a system sized precisely for your needs — no over-selling, no guesswork.

  • Custom system sizing based on your bill history
  • 3D layout design and load calculations
  • Transparent proposal with clear cost breakdown
SERVICE 02

Premium Quality Solar Products

We work only with trusted, certified manufacturers for panels, inverters and mounting structures — because a solar system is only as reliable as its weakest component.

  • Tier-1 solar panels with strong performance warranties
  • High-efficiency string & hybrid inverters
  • Corrosion-resistant mounting structures
  • BIS-approved components throughout
SERVICE 03

Professional Installation

Our trained, safety-certified installation crews handle everything from mounting to wiring, following strict quality checklists at every stage of the build.

  • Certified installation teams with safety training
  • Minimal disruption to your daily routine
  • Clean, code-compliant electrical wiring
  • Site cleanup after project completion
SERVICE 04

Commissioning & Testing

Before handover, every system undergoes rigorous testing to confirm performance, safety and grid-compliance — so it works exactly as designed from day one.

  • Full electrical & performance testing
  • Net-metering coordination with the utility
  • Safety compliance verification
  • Complete documentation & handover

Performance Monitoring
