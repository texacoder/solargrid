About the Scheme

Making Rooftop Solar More Affordable

The government's rooftop solar subsidy scheme is designed to accelerate residential solar adoption by directly reducing the upfront cost of installation. Eligible households can receive subsidy support based on system capacity, credited directly through empanelled vendors like SolarGrid.

  • ✓ Direct subsidy transfer for eligible households
  • ✓ Applicable for grid-connected rooftop systems
  • ✓ We handle documentation & portal registration
  • ✓ Net metering coordination included
