Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from homeowners, businesses and farms exploring solar.

Solar panels convert sunlight into DC electricity, which an inverter converts to AC power usable in your home or business. Any excess power can be exported to the grid through net metering, and you draw from the grid when your system isn't generating enough — like at night.

As a rough guide, 1 kW of solar capacity needs about 80–100 sq. ft. of shadow-free roof area. Our team conducts a free site visit to assess your exact roof space and recommend the ideal system size.

Cost depends on system size, component quality and site complexity. After the government subsidy is applied, net costs for residential systems typically become significantly more affordable. Request a free quote for an accurate, itemized estimate.

Most grid-connected residential rooftop systems are eligible, subject to current government guidelines. Our team checks your eligibility, handles portal registration, and manages the entire application on your behalf. Visit our Government Subsidy page for current slabs.

Payback periods vary with system size, subsidy amount and your electricity usage, but most residential systems pay for themselves well within the product's operational lifetime — after which the electricity generated is essentially free.

Most residential installations are completed within a few days once materials are on-site, following approval and design finalization. Larger commercial or industrial projects take longer depending on scale and complexity.

Standard grid-tied systems shut down during outages for safety. If backup during outages matters to you, we can design a hybrid system with battery storage that keeps essential loads running.

Yes — we manage the full process, including utility approvals, net metering application, and coordination with the electricity board, so you don't have to navigate the paperwork yourself.

Solar systems are low-maintenance. Periodic panel cleaning and an annual inspection are usually enough to keep performance optimal. We offer AMC plans that cover scheduled cleaning, inspection and priority support.

Panels typically come with long-term performance warranties from manufacturers, while inverters carry separate product warranties. We also offer extended service support plans — full details are shared with your project proposal.

Our performance monitoring service flags underperformance early. If an issue is detected, our maintenance team investigates and resolves it — covered under warranty where applicable.
